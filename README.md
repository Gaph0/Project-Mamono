# Project Mamono

Core mod of the Mamono Project, for RimWorld 1.6. It adds mamonos: women carrying
the Mamono Lord's "Inma", who feed on the essence of men, break a man's will with
a touch, claim mates, and remake women into monsters.

- **The Mamono gene.** It forces its holder female, cures the ailments of age,
  keeps her fertile at every age, runs her body on mana, and turns her melee
  strikes into teasing instead of wounds.
- **Mana and essence.** Her mana drains away over two days, and only a man's
  essence refills it. Empty, she starves, collapses, and stops at nothing to feed.
- **Willpower and the tsugai bond.** Enough teasing breaks a man's will and leaves
  him downed, awake and helpless. A broken man can be claimed as a lifelong mate,
  which steels the will of both partners.
- **Corruption and incubisation.** A woman can be infused with mana until she
  awakens as a monster of her infuser's kind, and a man who is fed on often enough
  slowly becomes an incubus.

**Requires:** Harmony, Biotech, Big and Small - Framework, ISEKAI RPG Leveling, Modlist Configurator

**Optional:** Ideology (the monster extremists meme, its precepts and its two rites), Vanilla Psycasts Expanded (monster xenotypes can spawn as psycasters), yayo's Animation (the bonding and feeding animations), Intimacy - Friends n' Lovers (lovin' feeds her)

## Content

Mamonos live among the world's other pawns: as raiders, as visitors, as wild women
who can be tamed, and as colonists of your own. A mamono who goes hungry will look
for a man to feed on, and the women around her are prey of a different kind. Every
other mod in the family builds its own species on this one: a mamono from any of
them carries the gene and follows every rule below.

### Xenotypes

- **Mamono** - the base monster, and the xenotype a woman becomes when nothing
  chooses a species for her. Her daughters inherit her whole gene set and are
  always female.

### Genes

- `Mamono` - forces her female, cures and blocks the ailments of age, keeps her
  fertile and never sterile, spares her all nudity thoughts, gives her the mana
  need, and turns her melee strikes into tease damage. Out of the gene packs: a
  trader or a quest cannot hand it out.
- `Mamono venom` - her melee strikes inject a non-lethal venom that slows a victim
  down. It wears off after 4 hours.
- `Fiery mamono` - she is immune to fire, heat and lava. Her bonded mate gains a
  lesser ward: a comfort ceiling 40 °C higher, and less flammable and less
  vulnerable to flame.
- `Mamono claws` - +15% tease damage, −15% manipulation.
- `flight` - wings, a long leap that needs no line of sight and clears water, and
  a caravan that travels faster and carries extra riders.
- `weak-winged flight` - the same leap and the same easy ground, but the wings are
  too small to carry a rider.
- `large frame` - for a mamono who is already large: a caravan with her in it moves
  20% faster, and she counts as a mount. It cannot sit in the same woman as a
  flight gene.

### Needs: mana and essence

- **Mana** (mamonos only) - a full bar drains away over 2 days, and eating restores
  only a little of it. A caravan's travel pauses the drain entirely. A mamono fills
  her bar from a man's essence, either through the give and drain orders or through
  lovin'. At empty she starves in three stages - drained, desperate, collapsed -
  and the starvation is never lethal.
- **Essence** (ordinary humans) - regenerates from empty to full in a day. Every
  transfer gives both partners a +5 mood for a day and pays experience, and an
  intimate transfer queues lovin' when a bed is free.

### Teasing and willpower

Willpower is a capacity on the Health tab, hidden on a mamono's own card. Her melee
strikes deal tease damage instead of wounds, and tease damage eats willpower until
the victim's will breaks at one tenth of it: they collapse, awake and helpless, and
stay that way until the teasing fades. Your own mamonos carry a "Tease attacks"
switch on their inspect pane; turned off, they wound normally again, though their
teasing abilities keep teasing.

### Tsugai bonds

A tsugai is the mate bond between a mamono and a man, shown on the Social tab.

- **Forced.** Knocking out a bondable man with tease lets her claim him: a short
  ritual that seals the bond and drains what is left of his essence into her mana.
  A bonded wild mamono is tamed on the spot.
- **Voluntary.** Mamonos and men propose to partners they desire, and it costs the
  man half his essence bar. Your own colonists do not propose: only outsiders do,
  and only to each other.
- **What it does.** More willpower for both, opinion and romance floored at the
  top, feeding from a bonded partner only, a mood lift that grows with the number
  of bonds, and no jealousy between pawns who share a mate.
- **When it ends.** A dead partner severs it: the survivor grieves, loses willpower
  for the grief's duration, and carries a −20 mood for 30 days. Bringing the dead
  mate back lifts the grief but does not restore the bond.

### Corruption and incubisation

- **Corruption** turns a woman into a monster. A mamono pours her mana into a
  downed woman, one infusion at a time, and the last infuser decides what she
  becomes. Corruption fades while the victim is up and fighting, so only a
  sustained effort finishes the change. A mamono may also simply offer the change
  to an upright woman and do the whole thing in one ceremony.
- **Incubisation** is the male counterpart. A man who is fed on becomes an incubus
  in stages, keeping his human body and gaining more of the perks at each stage:
  faster essence regen, less sleep, less hunger, and eventually ageless fertility.
  A full incubus also regains a little food from every transfer. The mark of the
  last mamono to feed him keeps other unbonded mamonos away from him.

### Monster extremists meme (Ideology)

"All men should be drained, and all women should be transformed." The meme holds
one opinion precept - believers admire mamonos and bonded men and look down on
adult humanlikes who are neither - and two rituals: the rite of awakening, which
turns a willing colonist into the ritual leader's own kind, and the rite of forced
awakening, which turns a prisoner into a random monster and halves her will and her
certainty in her old ideoligion. Its three varieties, relaxed, exalted and
strict, set how strong the opinions are. Without Ideology, none of it loads.

### Factions

Core adds no faction of its own. It does clear the vanilla selectable factions out
of world creation so a new world is not flooded with them, and it adds monster
girls to the Empire's and the Traders Guild's xenotype pools, so both of those
still spawn and still work as they always did.

### Settings

Everything is tunable under Options, Mod Settings, Project Mamono, on tabs for
ISEKAI, experience, mana, corruption, incubisation, the tsugai bond, bonded pawns,
animation and intimacy. Debug is the only hidden tab, and the intimacy tab appears
only while the Intimacy mod is loaded. A "Restore defaults" button resets the lot.
The monster extremists meme and the three bloodline genes have no tab: their values
are fixed.
